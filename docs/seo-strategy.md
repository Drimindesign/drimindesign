# SEO strategy: how Drimindesign gets found

Research date: November 2026. The keyword phrases come from Google's autocomplete for India. Autocomplete shows what people actually type, but not how often, so check volumes in the free tools below before choosing priorities.

## 1. What people search for

Clients search for a **service plus a place**, not for agency names:

| Service | Real searches (Google India autocomplete) |
|---|---|
| Branding | branding agency in Mumbai / Pune / Bangalore / Delhi / India, branding agency near me, branding agency for startups (in India) |
| Brand identity | brand identity design services, visual identity design services, corporate identity design services |
| Logo | logo design company India, logo design company in Ahmedabad / Chennai, logo and branding services |
| Website | website design company in Pune / Mumbai / Delhi, web design agency in India / Ahmedabad, website design for small business (cost) |
| E-commerce | ecommerce website development company in [city], ecommerce website design and development company in India |
| UI/UX and apps | ui ux design agency in India / Bangalore / Pune, ui ux design services in India, app design company in India |

### Pune: the phrases to win first

The studio is based in **Pune**, so these are the primary targets (also from Google autocomplete):

| Service | Pune searches |
|---|---|
| Branding | branding agency in Pune, branding company in Pune, design agency in Pune, creative agency in Pune, design studio Pune |
| Website | website design company in Pune, web design company Pune, website design and development company in Pune, best / top website design company in Pune |
| UI/UX and apps | ui ux design agency in Pune, ui ux design studio in Pune, best ui ux design companies in Pune |
| Packaging | packaging design agency in Pune, packaging design studio in Pune |
| Logo and graphics | logo design company in Pune, graphic design company / studio in Pune |
| E-commerce | ecommerce website development company in Pune |

Two conclusions:

1. **Lead with Pune, then India.** Almost every phrase ends in a city or "near me". Pune is now in the home title, the H1's hidden text, the Contact page and the structured data. Each future service page should target "[service] in Pune" first.
2. **"Drimindesign" has no search demand yet.** Google even suggests "dreamina" for "drimin". That's normal for a young studio: people find you through what you do, and the brand name grows from there.

## 2. What's already done on the site

- Every page has a unique title, a 140–160 character description, a canonical URL and Open Graph tags.
- The home page title is "Drimindesign — Branding, Web & App Design Agency in Pune". The hidden text of the H1 says what the studio does and where; the giant wordmark looks the same.
- The Contact page says "Based in Pune, with clients across India and worldwide".
- Project pages are titled by the work, e.g. "Breeze and Mist: Brand identity".
- Structured data: Organization (with logo, email, services, Pune address, areas served: Pune, India and worldwide, alternate spellings of the name), WebSite, CreativeWork and BreadcrumbList on projects, ContactPage on Contact.
- `sitemap.xml` lists every page, and `robots.txt` points to it.
- `/sitemap.html` is a plain-links page for people and crawlers, linked in the footer.
- The site is fast, server-rendered and works without JavaScript, all of which Google rewards.

## 3. Next on-site steps (in order of impact)

1. **A Services page, then one page per service**: `/services/branding` ("Branding agency in Pune"), `/services/website-design` ("Website design company in Pune"), `/services/ui-ux-app-design` ("UI/UX design agency in Pune"), `/services/packaging-design` ("Packaging design agency in Pune"). Each needs 400–800 words of real copy (what you deliver, process, timeline, starting price, FAQs), links to the matching projects, and a contact button. One page per search intent is how agencies rank for "[service] agency in Pune".
2. **Pune is in place** on the home page, Contact and the structured data. The legal pages still say disputes go to "the courts with jurisdiction over the studio's place of business"; have a lawyer confirm whether to name Pune there.
3. **Richer case studies.** Each project page should say the problem, your role, the result (numbers if possible) and the client's words. Long, original case studies rank for long-tail searches like "pharmacy branding" or "resort brand identity".
4. **FAQ sections** on the Services pages ("How much does a logo cost in India?", "How long does a website take?"). These match question searches and can show as rich results.
5. **A project-specific share image** is already set for each project. Add one branded share image for the home page (`app/opengraph-image.tsx`) so shares on WhatsApp and LinkedIn look professional.

## 4. Off-site: where most early rankings come from

1. **Google Business Profile** (free, the most important for "near me" and "in Pune" searches). Create it as a service-area business covering Pune if you don't want your address public. Use the category "Graphic designer" or "Website designer", add services, photos of work and the website link.
2. **Reviews.** Ask every happy client for a Google review. Reviews are a top local ranking factor.
3. **Portfolio platforms with a link back to the site:** Behance, Dribbble, LinkedIn company page, Instagram. Post each case study and link to its page on drimindesigns.co.in.
4. **Directories:** Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush and The Manifest (agency listings that rank on page one for "top branding agencies in India"), plus Justdial and Sulekha (list the business under Pune).
5. **Client credits:** ask clients to add "Website by Drimindesign" with a link in their footer. Each one is a relevant backlink.
6. **Consistent name, email and website everywhere.** Use the same spelling and details on every profile.

## 6. A monthly routine

- **Week 1:** check Search Console: which searches you appear for, and pages with many views but few clicks (improve their title or description).
- **Week 2:** publish one piece: a new case study or a guide (e.g. "Branding cost in India: what you pay for").
- **Week 3:** get one or two links: a directory, a client credit or a Behance post.
- **Week 4:** ask for reviews and answer existing ones.

Expect the first movement in 2–3 months and steady leads in 6–12. SEO compounds slowly. Google Ads for "[service] agency in Pune" can bring leads in the meantime.
