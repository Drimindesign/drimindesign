# Idea to Orbit: story and fight design

The rule for every level: **one new idea, taught, tested, then twisted.** No two fights share a format.
Difficulty rises by asking new things of the player, not by adding more of the same enemy.

## What it's built on (published, proven designs)

| Source | The lesson | Where it shows up here |
|---|---|---|
| **Nintendo's four-step level design** (kishōtenketsu, used across Mario) | Introduce a mechanic safely, develop it, twist it, conclude | Every level below has an *intro, develop, twist, finale* |
| **Plants vs. Zombies** | One new element per level; the roster grows gradually | One recruit and one enemy type per level |
| **Into the Breach / Dark Souls** | Telegraph every enemy attack: hard is fine if it's readable | Wind-ups before every heavy blow, charge or cannon shot (a glow, a shout, a stance) |
| **Left 4 Dead's "AI Director"** | Pace tension: build-up, peak, relax | Each level alternates quiet stretches, a rising threat, a peak fight, then a breather with a reward |
| **Clash of Clans** | A siege against a defended base; your army's mix counters its defences | The fortress siege (level 5) and the Copycat base (level 8) |
| **Hades** | Dying moves the story forward; characters remember | Camp conversations that change after every run, including failed ones |
| **Hollow Knight / Shadow of the Colossus** | Bosses are puzzles with phases, not big health bars | Each boss has three phases and a weakness that one teammate exploits |
| **Celeste** | Fair difficulty: generous checkpoints and quick retries | Checkpoint before every peak fight; under 3 seconds to retry |

## The story

**Kachujin** carries the Spark: a big idea that Mr. Grey wants to copy and own. His Copycats have taken the
people who could help her build it. She frees them one by one, and each one changes how she fights. At the
end the squad doesn't just escape Grey: they launch the idea into orbit, where no one can copy it.

Theme, quietly the studio's own: **original ideas, made real by a team.**

## Levels

### 1 · The Prison Break (rescue)
- **New:** sword combat, breaking a cage.
- **Recruit:** the **Knight**: caged in Grey's village; freed, he raises his shield over her as the guards rush in.
- **Intro:** one guard, alone, to learn the sword.
- **Develop:** two guards together; the Knight's shield blocks blows aimed at her.
- **Twist:** the alarm bell: guards pour in from both sides, so you must escape rather than fight everyone.
- **Finale:** reach the truck before the gate closes.
- **Difficulty:** low; there's always a way out.

### 2 · Into the Dark (survival, hold the line)
- **New:** the pack. Wolves circle and strike from the side you're not facing.
- **Recruit:** the **Ranger**: an arrow kills a wolf leaping at Kachujin before she sees who shot it.
- **Intro:** a lone wolf on the path, learning its lunge (it lowers its head, then lunges).
- **Develop:** a pack circles; the Ranger shoots from a tree while you hold the ground beneath her.
- **Twist:** the lantern starts to fail; wolves only fully show in its light.
- **Finale:** hold for 60 seconds until dawn breaks; the pack runs.
- **Difficulty:** reading the attack from behind, not raw damage.

### 3 · The River (decoy and escort)
- **New:** moving footing (logs, rafts).
- **Recruit:** the **Rogue**: already free, hiding among the logs; she needs Kachujin to make noise.
- **Intro:** cross drifting logs.
- **Develop:** a raft with a captive and guards; **you are the distraction**: draw guards to one end while the
  Rogue slips round and strikes from behind.
- **Twist:** the raft breaks apart mid-fight; fight on a shrinking floor.
- **Finale:** carry the captive (a Peasant Girl) to the far bank.
- **Difficulty:** split attention: position and timing.

### 4 · The Caves (light and dark)
- **New:** darkness. Enemies are only fully visible in torchlight; light the braziers to win ground.
- **Recruit:** the **Mage**: after a cave-in traps the squad, a light in the dark burns the rocks away.
- **Intro:** walk the dark with the torch; shapes move at the edge of the light.
- **Develop:** light braziers to hold safe zones; the Mage's area spell knocks a group back.
- **Twist:** the **Mutant** (Grey's first experiment) charges out of the dark and sends teammates flying.
- **Finale:** relight the great fire; the Mutant flees from the light.
- **Difficulty:** resource management (light) and reacting to the Mutant's telegraphed charge.

### 5 · The Fortress (siege)
- **New:** defences. Walls, archer posts and a cannon that fire on your squad.
- **Recruit:** the **Barbarian**: Grey's champion and the level's boss; beaten, he learns Grey burned his
  village and smashes the gate open for her.
- **Intro:** one archer post; take cover and send the Ranger.
- **Develop:** a cannon; it glows before firing, so move the squad out of its line, then rush it.
- **Twist:** the wall: only heavy blows break it, so the Knight shields the squad while you hit the gate.
- **Finale (boss, 3 phases):** shield (strike from behind with the Rogue), rage (dodge the overhead blows),
  kneel (spare him).
- **Difficulty:** army composition: which teammate goes where.

### 6 · The Mountain Pass (chase)
- **New:** fighting on the move. Grey's siege cart races downhill, and your truck chases it.
- **Recruit:** **Gorr**: a chained giant pulling the cart; cut his chains during the ambush.
- **Intro:** drive the truck alongside the cart.
- **Develop:** jump across to the cart and fight on it while it moves.
- **Twist:** an avalanche: leave the cart, run on foot, and Gorr carries the slowest teammate.
- **Finale:** Gorr slams the cart off the road.
- **Difficulty:** speed and spacing.

### 7 · The Copycat Lab (waves with rules)
- **New:** arena waves, each with a rule that changes how you fight:
  - only arrows hurt the shielded ones;
  - the floor shocks every 10 seconds, so jump;
  - healing is off.
- **Enemies:** Mutants and their copies (copies of your own teammates, wearing Grey's colours).
- **Finale:** destroy the copying machine while the copies attack.
- **Difficulty:** adapting to rules, not grinding.

### 8 · Grey's Tower (siege plus boss)
- **New:** the full Clash of Clans siege: walls, towers, cannons and a mortar.
  - Before it starts, you choose your **army mix** at the camp: copies of cheap troops (Rogue, Ranger) or
    a few heavy ones (Gorr, Barbarian).
- **Boss: Maw**, in 3 phases:
  - **grab:** Maw seizes a teammate; free them within 5 seconds;
  - **swallow the light:** fight in darkness again;
  - **the core:** Gorr slams Maw down, and everyone strikes.
- **Finale:** launch the Spark into orbit from the tower.

## Difficulty curve
Each level asks one new skill and reuses the earlier ones:

| Level | Main skill tested |
|---|---|
| 1 | Swordplay |
| 2 | Reading attacks from behind |
| 3 | Positioning and timing |
| 4 | Managing light |
| 5 | Army composition |
| 6 | Speed |
| 7 | Adapting to rules |
| 8 | All of it |

## Between levels: the camp
- Everyone she has met sits by the fire (Sitting Idle). Each has a new line after every run, including
  failed runs (the Hades idea).
- **Spend Spark Hearts** to level teammates permanently (the Clash of Clans idea).
- **Pick one of three upgrades** (exists now: Twin arrows, Healing guard, Wide slam; more per teammate later).

## Retention hooks
- **Stars per level:** save every prisoner, lose no teammate, beat the time.
- **Daily Expedition:** the same seeded level for everyone that day, with a shareable result card linking
  back to the site.
- **Projects as unlocks:** every star tier unlocks one of the studio's projects in the camp's gallery.
